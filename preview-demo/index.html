🔒 ZenGram — Distraction-Free Instagram

🎯 Engineered for Competitive Exam Focus

Exam preparation (JEE, NEET, GATE, UPSC) requires deep concentration, but you still need Instagram to message peers, attend group study calls, or review bookmarked formula sheets.

  • Zero Feed & Zero Explore: The infinite algorithmic post feed and discover rabbit hole are 100% blocked.
  • No Endless Reels Scroll: Reels feed is eradicated. If a study buddy sends a concept reel in chat, it opens in an isolated single-video player with no swipe-to-next!
  • Saved Section Intact: Open your formula cheat-sheets and diagrams via /username/saved/ and /p/post_id without distractions.
  • Voice & Video Calling: Integrated WebRTC calling with hardware camera/mic access right inside chat headers.

🎨 "Post & Ghost" for Serious Content Creators

The greatest enemy of a creator is consuming other people's content instead of producing their own. ZenGram enables true creation without algorithmic exhaustion.

  • Publish Directly: Dedicated support for /create and native photo/video upload file pickers. Upload your reels, carousels, and stories cleanly.
  • Client & Collab DMs: Manage sponsorship inquiries, collaborator messages, and audience replies in pure inbox mode.
  • Zero Brain Rot: Never lose 45 minutes falling into your own explore feed while checking comments or DM analytics.

🛡️ 100% Zero-Data-Theft Guarantee

ZenGram is strictly client-side and completely open source. Anyone can inspect every single line of code in this repository.

✓ Direct Meta SSL Connection: 100% of your requests travel directly to official Instagram endpoints (https://www.instagram.com). No proxies, no relays.

✓ Zero Middleman Servers: ZenGram operates entirely on your local machine / browser. We do NOT operate any server collecting credentials, cookies, tokens, or messages.

✓ Client-Side DOM Interception Only: We simply remove the addictive algorithmic UI elements from the DOM and sandbox in-chat video links.
